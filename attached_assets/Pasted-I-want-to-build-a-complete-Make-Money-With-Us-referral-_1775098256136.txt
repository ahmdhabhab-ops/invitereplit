I want to build a complete “Make Money With Us!” referral/affiliate program for my e-invite website.

GOAL:
Allow users to register, refer clients, and earn a 30% commission on every successful sale they bring.

---

## USER EXPERIENCE (FRONTEND):

Create a landing page titled:

“Make Money With Us!”

It should be attractive, modern, and persuasive. Explain clearly:

* Anyone can join for free
* They get a personal referral link
* They earn 30% commission on every successful purchase made through their link
* No limits on earnings

Make it exciting but realistic and trustworthy (not spammy or exaggerated).

---

## HOW IT WORKS (DISPLAY ON PAGE):

1. Sign up for free
2. Get your unique referral link
3. Share it with friends, couples, or on social media
4. Earn 30% commission every time someone purchases through your link
5. Track your earnings in your dashboard

---

## USER REGISTRATION:

Users must create an account with:

* Full name
* Email
* Password

After signup:

* Generate a unique referral ID/link (example: website.com/?ref=USER123)
* Save user in database

---

## REFERRAL TRACKING:

* When someone visits the site using a referral link:

  * Store referral ID (via cookies or localStorage)

* When that visitor makes a purchase:

  * Link the sale to the referring user
  * Record commission (30% of sale amount)

---

## USER DASHBOARD:

Each registered user should have a dashboard showing:

* Total earnings
* Number of referrals
* Successful sales
* Pending commissions
* Paid commissions
* Their unique referral link (with copy button)

---

## ADMIN PANEL:

Admin should be able to:

* View all users
* View all referrals and sales
* See commissions generated
* Mark commissions as “paid”
* Adjust commission rate (default 30%)

---

## COMMISSION SYSTEM:

* Commission = 30% of total sale
* Status:

  * Pending (after sale)
  * Approved
  * Paid

---

## ANTI-ABUSE:

* Prevent users from referring themselves
* Basic fraud checks (same email/IP warnings)
* Validate real purchases only

---

## UI / DESIGN:

* Clean, modern, and wedding-themed
* Use trust-building elements:

  * “No upfront cost”
  * “Real earnings”
  * “Simple process”

---

## MARKETING TEXT (IMPORTANT):

Include persuasive but honest messaging like:

* “Turn your network into income”
* “Earn 30% on every successful referral”
* “Perfect for wedding planners, influencers, and anyone who knows engaged couples”
* “No experience needed”

Avoid unrealistic claims. Keep it professional and trustworthy.

---

## TECH STACK:

* Frontend: HTML/CSS/JavaScript (or React)
* Backend: Node.js (Express)
* Database: MongoDB or SQL

---

## IMPORTANT:

* System must be fully functional end-to-end
* Clean and modular code
* Easy to integrate into existing website
* Secure handling of referral tracking and commissions

Please build the full system and explain how to integrate and deploy it.
